## Muthyala ❤️ Mothina

**Muthyala ❤️ Mothina** is a beautifully designed, responsive wedding invitation website that guides guests through the couple’s story and event details. The landing page presents two tactile cards—**Groom’s Invite** and **Bride’s Invite**—linking to dedicated pages for each side. Visitors are greeted with a rich, dark-themed palette, elegant typography, and subtle animations. Upon arrival, a welcoming modal explains that background music will play, and guests can enable sound. The site culminates in heartfelt invitation text, photo galleries, and contact information, all packaged in a modern, accessible layout.

## Design & Aesthetics

* **Dark Palette**:

  * Background: `#49243E`
  * Footer: `#704264`
  * Headings: `#BB8493`
  * Body Text: `#DBAFA0`
* **Typography**:

  * Headings: *Rye* (classic serif)
  * Body & UI: *Anek Latin* (legible sans-serif)
  * Special accents: *Ponnala* and *Lakki Reddy* for cultural flair
* **Tactile Neumorphism**: Cards appear raised by default with dual shadows, then “press” inward on hover or click using inset shadows and downward translation.
* **Animations**:

  * Section reveal on scroll
  * Smooth modal opening for sound unlock
  * Button press feedback on cards

## Technology Stack

* **HTML5**: Semantic structure and accessibility
* **CSS3 & SCSS**: Custom theming, responsive layouts, neumorphic shadows
* **Bootstrap 5**: Grid system, modals, utilities
* **jQuery**: DOM manipulation, event handling
* **JavaScript**: Audio control, scroll-reveal interactions
* **Google Fonts**: “Rye,” “Anek Latin,” “Ponnala,” and “Lakki Reddy”
* **Bootstrap Icons**: Mute/unmute and interactive icons

## Usage

1. **Clone or download** the repo to your web server directory.
2. **Open** `index.html` in your browser.
3. **Allow sound** via the “Proceed” modal to enjoy the background music.
4. **Click** either “Groom’s Invite” or “Bride’s Invite” to view side-specific pages.
5. **Scroll** through sections to see animated reveals of details and images.
